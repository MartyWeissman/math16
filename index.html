The weekly rhythm

About 300 students and a teaching team of sixteen: Marty (instructor), four TAs, eight Active Learning Facilitators (ALFs — advanced undergraduates who help run the discussion sections), two people from ACE (Academic Excellence Program), and a tutor from Learning Support Services. Two lectures and two discussion sections a week; Fridays belong to the teaching team.

Monday

Lecture

~300 students with Marty, Media Theatre 110.

Tuesday

Section

~40 students with a TA and ALFs, McHenry 1st floor. Bring your worksheet, printed and completed.

Wednesday

Lecture

~300 students with Marty, Media Theatre 110.

Thursday

Section

~40 students with a TA and ALFs, McHenry 1st floor. Bring your lecture digest, handwritten.

Friday

Teaching team

Teaching-team meeting — no students. Occasional checkpoint deadlines.
